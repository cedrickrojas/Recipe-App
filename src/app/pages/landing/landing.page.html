A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Simple answers.

What can I save in my collection?+

Add a recipe name, category, ingredients, instructions, and preparation time. You can edit your recipes as you make them your own.

Do I need an account?+

Yes. Create an account with your email and password to save and access your personal collection.

Can I use it on my phone?+

Absolutely. Your collection works in your phone’s browser, so you can keep the ingredients and instructions close while you cook.

Is this a collection of recipes to browse?+

This is a home for your own recipes. The sample collection above gives you a taste of the possibilities; your personal collection begins with the recipes you add.
